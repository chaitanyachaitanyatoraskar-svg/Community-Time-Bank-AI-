1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy

Community Time Bank AI is a full-stack, peer-to-peer service reciprocity platform designed for collegiate and local communities. The platform enables individuals to exchange practical skills, neighborly assistance, and academic tutoring using Time Credits rather than monetary currency.

The Non-Negotiable Reciprocity Principle: One verified hour of community service equals exactly one base Time Credit (1.0 cr), regardless of the conventional commercial market value of the skill. The system eliminates financial friction, empowering students and local residents to access high-value assistance through reciprocal contribution.
  • Dual-Party Atomic Confirmation: Credits never transfer automatically upon session completion. Both the requester and helper must submit cryptographic completion confirmations in their portal before balance settlement occurs.
  • Fair Credit Multiplier System: Standardized 1.0×, 1.25×, and 1.5× complexity multipliers accommodate tasks demanding certified expertise, physical hazards, or specialized equipment without compromising fairness.
  • Non-Monetary Community Utility: Surplus credits can be gifted to a shared Community Credit Pool for hardship grants or redeemed in a Local Perks Catalog for event passes and cafe vouchers. Direct fiat cash withdrawals are strictly forbidden.

4. Core Algorithms & Mathematical Formulations

4.1 Explainable Recommendation Engine (Matching Algorithm)

When a member posts a request or searches for assistance, the recommendation engine in matchingService.ts evaluates all active helpers on a 100-point scale:

MatchScore = S_skill (40%) + S_avail (25%) + S_location (15%) + S_rating (10%) + S_reliability (10%)
1. Skill Relevance (Max 40 pts)

Direct skill match awards points scaled by proficiency: Expert = 40 pts, Intermediate = 32 pts, Beginner = 24 pts. Secondary matching skills add +4 pts each. Partial category/keyword matches receive a 20 pt baseline.

2. Availability Overlap (Max 25 pts)

Exact match on requester's preferred day awards 25 pts. General active schedule slots award 15 pts. Unscheduled helpers receive 5 pts baseline.

3. Location Proximity (Max 15 pts)

Identical neighborhood or remote-compatible tasks award 15 pts. Adjacent neighborhoods within the municipal area receive 7 pts.

4. Trust & Reliability (Max 20 pts)

Rating score (Max 10 pts) equals (Rating / 5.0) × 10. Past completion reliability (Max 10 pts) awards 10 pts for ≥ 5 completed sessions, 7 pts for 1–4 completed sessions.

4.2 Fair Credit Multiplier System

TransferAmount = ServiceDurationInHours × AgreedMultiplier
  • 1.0× STANDARD General tutoring, language exchange, plant care, conversational assistance.
  • 1.25× INTERMEDIATE Certified instruction, complex software debugging, moderate physical effort.
  • 1.5× ADVANCED CAP Component-level hardware repair, hazardous tools, emergency support. Strictly capped at 1.5×.

5. Application Features & User Routes

5.1 Public Interfaces

  • / (Landing Page): Editorial manifesto, live Fair Credit Multiplier Calculator sandbox, 5 exchange principles, and interactive persona launcher.
  • /auth (Authentication): Login and registration forms with a 1-click persona bar for instant evaluator switching without manual typing.
  • /terms & /privacy: Complete regulatory, governance, data safety, and non-monetary compliance documentation.

5.2 Authenticated Member Workspace

  • /dashboard: Operational control hub displaying live credit balance, completed exchange count, upcoming bookings timeline, and AI helper recommendations.
  • /find-help: Service marketplace with multi-tier filters, category search, proximity matching, and instantaneous booking requests.
  • /my-listings: Listing manager allowing members to create, edit, pause, and close help offers and requests with complexity tier selection.
  • /bookings: Bilateral lifecycle manager tracking sessions from requested to completed, featuring mutual completion confirmations.
  • /wallet: Transparent ledger tracking personal balance, total earned, total spent, filterable transaction histories, and breakdown receipts.
  • /redeem: Perks and vouchers catalog for non-monetary credit redemptions with cryptographic voucher generation.
  • /community-pool: Solidarity reserve enabling members to donate surplus hours with dedication messages and view public aid distributions.
  • /profile: Personal skills manager, proficiency tag selector, contact preferences, and recurring availability calendar.
  • /messages: In-app direct communication interface between exchange participants.
  • /community-events: Local group activities, community gardens, repair cafes, and workshop calendar.
  • /ai-assistant: Natural language concierge answering reciprocity policy queries and recommending helpers.

5.3 Administrative Command Center (AdminRoute)

  • /admin (Member Moderation): Directory search, balance inspection, and account suspension/reactivation.
  • /admin (Policy Governance): Category creation, multiplier eligibility toggles, and maximum multiplier ceiling configuration.
  • /admin (Dispute Resolution): Review flagged bookings, inspect communication logs, and execute binding credit adjustments.
  • /admin (Pool Distributions): Allocate hardship assistance from the shared community pool with mandatory immutable audit reasons.
  • /admin (Voucher Audit): Partner voucher validation and inventory restocking.
